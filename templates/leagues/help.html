⚽ 1. How the Fantasy League Works

This fantasy football league is designed around one unique and fun rule: every player chooses one team to win and one team to lose in each UK division.

The Golden Scoring Formula
Player Score = (Points of Winner Team) − (Points of Loser Team)
Points are taken directly from the official BBC Sport football tables for all 4 English leagues (Premier League, Championship, League One, League Two).
Example:
Division Your Winner Team Winner Pts Your Loser Team Loser Pts Your Net Score
Premier League Arsenal 24 Southampton 4 +20
Championship Leeds United 22 Portsmouth 9 +13
Total Score (Sum across divisions) +33 pts

📅 2. Workflow: Starting a New Season (August)

Follow these 3 easy steps whenever a new football season begins.

1 Open the Admin Hub

Click "League Admin Hub" in the top navigation bar.

2 Click "Start New Season"

Click the blue "Start New Season" button in the top right corner.

  • Enter a title (e.g. Season 2026/27 or 2027 Winter League).
  • Toggle "Set as current active season" so the BBC scraper automatically routes fresh scores to this season.
  • Click "Create & Proceed to Roster".
3 Add Any New Friends (Players)

If a new player joins the group, enter their name in the "Add New Player" card on the right side of the Admin Hub.

👥 3. Workflow: Enrolling Players & Setting Team Picks

How to enroll players and assign their winner/loser predictions for each division.

1 Select Participants

On the Roster & Team Picks screen, look at the "Select Participants" box on the right. Simply tick the checkbox for each friend taking part this year, and click "Save Enrolled Players".

2 Click "Configure Picks"

Next to each player's name, click the blue "Configure Picks" button.

3 Pick Winner & Loser Teams

For each division, use the dropdowns to pick:

  • Winner Team (Team expected to finish high and score lots of points).
  • Loser Team (Team expected to struggle at the bottom).

Safety Rule: You cannot select the same team as both winner and loser in the same division. The form will show a clear red message if this happens.

Click "Save Picks" when finished. Repeat this for each player.

🔒 4. Workflow: Locking Picks (Kickoff Day)

Once Gameweek 1 kicks off, lock the picks to freeze all choices and prevent accidental changes.

1 Click "Lock Picks Now"

In the Roster & Selections page, click the yellow "Lock Picks Now" button in the top right header.

What happens when picks are locked?
  • The badge turns red: Picks Locked 🔒.
  • All "Edit picks" links are safely hidden from the scoreboard and player cards.
  • Nobody can alter selections during the season.
  • You can unlock them at any time by simply clicking "Unlock Picks" if an honest correction is needed.

🔄 5. Workflow: Refreshing Live Scores

Scores are retrieved directly from BBC Sport tables.

1 One-Click Refresh

Anytime games have finished, simply click the "Refresh Scores" button in the top dark navigation bar.

The system fetches the latest standings and recalculates all fantasy scores instantly. A green confirmation banner will appear showing how many teams were updated.

2 Inspect Real Tables

Click "Real Tables" in the top menu to view the complete official BBC tables (showing matches played, won, drawn, lost, goal differences, and points).

🏛️ 6. Workflow: Archiving a Completed Season (May)

How to safely wrap up a finished championship without losing any data.

1 Final Refresh

After the last game of the season has been played, click "Refresh Scores" so the final standings are fetched.

2 Click "Archive Season"

In the League Admin Hub, click the red "Archive Season" button next to the active season.

Data Permanently Preserved

The season is now permanently frozen in history. You and your friends can always look back at who won the league in previous years by selecting the season in the "Seasons" dropdown at the top of the page.
